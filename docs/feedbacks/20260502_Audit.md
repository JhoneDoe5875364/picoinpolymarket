## 1. Market List Section

### 1.1 Current strengths

- Clean card layout
- Clear Yes / No split
- Volume and end date visible
- Categories across the top

> I really like the look of it. I don’t mind the colour change, and the contrast is strong in both light and dark modes.

The market list already feels approachable, which is important. A new user should be able to land on the page and understand within seconds:

- what the prediction is,
- what Yes means,
- what No means,
- when the market ends,
- how active the market is.

### 1.2 Recommended additions

- **[ADDED]** The next improvement should be making each market card feel more “alive.” The market list should not just look like a directory of markets. It should communicate activity, urgency, and confidence.
- **[ADDED]** Add “Trending,” “New,” and “Hot” labels directly on market cards where relevant.
- **[ADDED]** Add small activity signals such as “24 trades today,” “12 comments,” “ending soon,” or “volume up 38%.”
- **[ADDED]** Add a compact sparkline or mini price/history movement line where possible.
- **[ADDED]** Add a “Featured Market” or rotating card at the top of the market list, similar to Polymarket’s highlighted/revolving market area.
- **[ADDED]** Add an “Ending Soon” filter because urgency increases interaction.
- **[ADDED]** Add a “Most Discussed” filter once comments exist.

The categories across the top are useful, but they should eventually behave less like static filters and more like discovery paths.

> **NOTE:** We have to make sure that any and all pictures used are non-copyright.

### 1.3 Suggested category structure

- Trending
- New
- Hot
- Ending Soon
- Sports
- Crypto
- Politics / Public Events
- Entertainment
- Community

### 1.4 Suggested definitions

| Label | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **New** | Created in the last 48–72 hours |
| **Trending** | High trade/comment activity in the last 24 hours compared with its previous baseline |
| **Hot** | Combination of high volume, high trade count, comment activity, and recent price movement |
| **Ending Soon** | Market closes within the next 24–72 hours |

- **[ADDED]** “Trending” should be based on recent activity, not total lifetime volume. A market with 10 trades in the last hour should feel hotter than a market with high old volume but no recent action.

## 2. Market Rules / Context Section

The rules/context area should be more visible, easier to digest, and specific to each market. The rules are not just informational. They are a trust layer. Users need to know exactly what they are predicting before they send Pi.

### 2.1 Recommended structure

- **Market Question** — A plain-English version of the market (e.g. “Will Team X win the championship by June 30, 2026?”)
- **What Counts as Yes** — Clear criteria for a Yes resolution.
- **What Counts as No** — Clear criteria for a No resolution.
- **Resolution Source** — Where the final result will come from (e.g. official league website, public blockchain data, verified news source, official government source, platform admin decision using listed sources)
- **Resolution Date** — The expected date or condition for resolving the market.
- **Edge Cases** — A short explanation of what happens if the event is cancelled, delayed, ambiguous, or unresolved.

### 2.2 Additional rules UX

- **[ADDED]** This section should be visually separated from the trade action area. The user should not be able to place a prediction without having easy access to the market’s resolution terms.
- **[ADDED]** For trust, each market should have a “Resolution Rules” card using the same structure every time: Question, Yes Criteria, No Criteria, Source, Close Time, Resolution Time, Edge Cases.
- **[ADDED]** Add a small “Why this matters” or “Market context” paragraph, but keep it neutral. Avoid language that pushes users toward Yes or No.

## 3. Trade Panel

### 3.1 Current strengths

- Price clarity
- Fee transparency
- Potential profit shown
- Easy to understand for new users

The trade panel is structurally solid. It is easy to use and understand for someone reaching the platform for the first time.

### 3.2 Improvements needed

The biggest improvement should be how it feels after interaction. Specifically:

- Clearer distinction between cost, payout, and net result
- Feedback after a trade action
- Confirmation/state change after prediction is submitted
- Precise and consistent calculations — This is critical for trust.

### 3.3 Terminology

- **[ADDED]** The wording “potential profit” may need to be replaced later for regulatory reasons. Safer alternatives: “Possible return,” “Estimated result,” “If correct, you may receive,” “Projected payout,” “Outcome estimate.”
- **[ADDED]** Do not mix “cost,” “payout,” “profit,” and “return” casually. Each term should have one meaning across the entire app.

**Suggested terminology**

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Amount** | The Pi the user is placing on the prediction |
| **Fee** | Platform fee |
| **Total Cost** | Amount + fee |
| **Estimated Return** | What the user may receive if correct |
| **Net Result** | Estimated return minus total cost |
| **Loss if Incorrect** | Total cost |

### 3.4 Calculation breakdown (before confirmation)

- **[ADDED]** The trade panel should show a clear calculation breakdown before the user confirms. Example: Prediction amount: 10 Pi / Platform fee: 0.2 Pi / Total cost: 10.2 Pi / If correct: 18.4 Pi estimated return / Net result after cost: 8.2 Pi / If incorrect: 0 Pi returned
- **[ADDED]** If calculations are even slightly inconsistent between market card, trade panel, profile, and admin, users will lose trust immediately.

### 3.5 Trade confirmation

After a user submits a prediction, the app should not simply reset or move on.

- **[ADDED]** Add a confirmation state after every completed action: “Prediction submitted successfully.” Then show: market name, Yes/No side selected, amount sent, fee, transaction/reference ID, wallet/payment status, expected resolution date, link to view position in profile.
- **[ADDED]** Add a loading state while Pi payment is being confirmed. Users should never wonder whether the click worked. Suggested states: Preparing payment → Awaiting Pi confirmation → Payment detected → Position recorded → Prediction confirmed.
- **[ADDED]** Add failure states with clear recovery instructions: Payment cancelled / Payment pending / Payment detected but position not recorded / Position recorded but confirmation delayed / Network error. This is especially important because Pi Browser and wallet flows can be sensitive. If users think Pi disappeared or the app froze, trust will collapse.

## 4. Comment Section

There is interest in adding a comment section on the trade panel or market page. This can be added later, but it is likely important for engagement.

- **[ADDED]** I would not put the full comment section inside the trade panel itself. The trade panel should stay focused on the action. Instead, add a “Discussion” section below the market details, with a comment count visible near the trade panel.

### 4.1 Recommended layout

1. Market question at top
2. Chart / activity / context
3. Trade panel on right or below on mobile
4. Rules card
5. Discussion section
6. Related markets

### 4.2 Comment controls (day one)

- Report comment
- Hide/delete from admin
- User mute/ban
- Pinned admin clarification
- “Official resolution note” from admin
- **[ADDED]** Add an “Admin Note” or “Market Clarification” feature separate from user comments. This protects trust if a market needs clarification before resolution.

## 5. Admin Panel

### 5.1 Current strengths

- Market management
- Suggestions pipeline
- User controls
- Metrics

> I am loving all of this. Data is everything, so the more we can capture, the better. We need a clear picture of everything happening on the admin page.

- **[ADDED]** The admin panel should not only manage markets. It should help detect risk, trust issues, payment issues, and user behavior patterns. The admin panel should become the safety center.

### 5.2 Recommended admin metrics

**Market metrics**

- Total volume
- Yes volume
- No volume
- Number of unique users
- Number of predictions
- Average prediction size
- Largest prediction
- Close date
- Resolution status
- Unresolved markets
- Markets with disputes/comments

**User metrics**

- Total users
- Active users today / week / month
- Users with open positions
- Users with resolved positions
- Repeat users
- Largest users by activity
- Users with failed/pending payments
- Flagged users

**Payment / wallet metrics**

- User-to-App payments received
- App-to-User payouts sent
- Pending payouts
- Failed payouts
- Manual payout queue
- Wallet address connected
- Wallet address missing
- Payment mismatch warnings

**Trust / safety metrics**

- Disputed markets
- Markets with high comment activity
- Suspicious activity
- Duplicate users
- Large sudden trades
- Unresolved edge cases
- Manual admin overrides

### 5.3 Admin additions

- **[ADDED]** Add a “Payout Queue” even if payouts are not fully automated yet. Should show: user, market, outcome, amount owed, wallet address, payment status, transaction ID, and admin action.
- **[ADDED]** Add an audit log. Every important admin action should be recorded: market created, market edited, market resolved, payout generated, payout marked paid, user banned, comment removed, rules changed. This protects the platform if users question outcomes later.

## 7. Non-Escrow Pi Flow

We have been trying to work out how to run things non-escrow because that may reduce regulatory and custody risk. The preferred idea is: User makes a prediction → user sends Pi → market resolves → Pi goes back to the user’s wallet, not into an internal wallet balance.

> My view: This is the right instinct.

A deposit/withdraw system creates more friction, more liability, and more regulatory resemblance to an exchange, casino wallet, or custodial platform. If PredictPix can avoid holding a user-facing balance, that is cleaner from a product and trust perspective.

- **[ADDED]** The best near-term model is not “deposit and withdraw.” It is “pay per prediction, then receive direct payout if correct.”

### 7.1 Suggested flow

1. User chooses market
2. User selects Yes or No
3. User enters amount
4. App shows exact cost, fee, and estimated return
5. User confirms Pi payment
6. Pi payment is verified
7. Position is created
8. Market resolves
9. If user is correct, payout is sent directly to the user’s wallet
10. Profile shows full transaction history

### 7.2 Legal / product notes

- **[ADDED]** This still does not eliminate all regulatory risk. The product can still be viewed as event-based wagering, prediction-market trading, or binary outcome contracting depending on jurisdiction. But avoiding internal balances is likely cleaner than creating a stored-value wallet inside the app.
- **[ADDED]** The platform should avoid language like: deposit, withdraw, account balance, investment, trading profit, guaranteed return, cash out, or portfolio — until legal review is complete.

**Safer wording**

- prediction amount
- send Pi
- return
- resolved result
- payment history
- payout status
- estimated outcome
- activity history

**Riskier wording to avoid**

- deposit
- withdraw
- balance
- profit
- investment
- trading
- bankroll
- cash out
- yield

## 8. Overall Product Feel

I’d like to see more dynamic markets under: Trending, New, Hot. Basically, the platform needs signals of activity so people are not just looking at static lists.

I visited Polymarket recently and liked their revolving card at the top that highlights certain markets and shows movement/graph activity. I am not sure whether we can or want to copy that directly, but the idea is good: it creates a moving, active, dynamic feel.

- **[ADDED]** PredictPix needs to feel alive even when liquidity is still low. This is very important for a new prediction platform.

### 8.1 Ways to create activity without faking anything

- Featured market carousel
- Recently created markets
- Recently resolved markets
- Most discussed markets
- Biggest movement today
- Ending soon
- New suggestion submitted
- Admin-picked market of the day
- Community poll/prediction prompt
- “Watchlist” feature
- “Share this market” CTA

- **[ADDED]** Do not fake volume or fake activity. But do present real activity in a more exciting way.

## 10. Final Recommendation

PredictPix is moving in the right direction. The current feedback is positive on the visual structure, market list, trade panel, admin concept, and dashboard direction.

The biggest next leap is not just adding more features. The biggest leap is making the platform feel: trustworthy, precise, alive, transparent, safe to use.

### Three most important product principles

1. **No ambiguity around money/Pi** — Users must always know what they are sending, what the fee is, what they may receive, and what happens if they are wrong.
2. **No ambiguity around market outcomes** — Every market needs clear resolution rules before users participate.
3. **No dead screens** — Market pages, lists, and dashboards should show activity, movement, discussion, and status wherever possible.

- **[ADDED]** My strongest recommendation is to build the next version around the non-escrow, transaction-history model: users send Pi per prediction, positions are recorded, and payouts go directly back to the user’s wallet after resolution. Avoid internal balances unless legal review says it is safe. This keeps the product simpler, cleaner, and easier to explain.
